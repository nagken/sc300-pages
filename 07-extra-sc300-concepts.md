# Extra SC-300 Concepts

> Concepts that span multiple domains or appear inconsistently in the exam outline but show up on the test.

## Cross-tenant Access Settings (CTAS)

Tenant-wide policy for inbound/outbound B2B - trust other tenants' MFA, restrict accessible apps. Often confused with CA.

## Cross-tenant Sync

Mirror users from one Entra tenant into another - useful for M&A.

## Privileged Access Groups

PIM-managed Entra groups - assign role to group, eligible members activate.

## Microsoft Entra Verified ID

Decentralized identity - issue/verify W3C verifiable credentials.
